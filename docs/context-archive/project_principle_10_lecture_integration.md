**The change:** Phase 1 of integrating an Agourim staff-training lecture (Michal & Smadar, 2025) extended the design-principles framework from 9 to 10. Principle 10 — **"Flexibility in defining ways of participation"** — names three legitimate participation styles (active / passive / separate, with five operational sub-categories: active, passive-in-space, passive-adjacent, separate-in-room, separate-at-home) and explicitly forbids "not cooperating" framing.

**Why:** The lecture was delivered to the whole Agourim staff and Yon attended as workshop educator. The workshop's 9-principle framework had implicit elements of the lecture's insights (Principles 2, 5, 8) but no explicit taxonomy of participation styles, no per-student anchor logging, and no coordinated facilitator acknowledgment scripts. The lecture was grounded in two case examples from the same school (a student with English-on-sofa anchor; a student needing 2-hour morning rest) — directly relevant clinical practice, not academic corpus.

**How to apply:**
- Principle 10 is the newest and the only one grounded in clinical practice rather than verified corpus. It carries an honest caveat ("piloted practices, not evidence-based interventions"). The master-document Honest Limitations section should reflect this alongside the Principle 7 caveat.
- Per-student anchor logs live at `Arduino_Projects/Project_1_Light_Signals/teacher_materials/student_anchor_log.html` + `_he.html` — fillable one-pager per student.
- Structured acknowledgment scripts at `teacher_materials/acknowledgment_scripts.html` + `_he.html` — 8 categories A–H including passive-adjacent (students present but declining to engage).
- The Michal & Smadar 2025 lecture is logged in `Verification_Log.md` as row NA1 under a non-article-evidence-sources heading — same convention any future internal-source citation should follow.
